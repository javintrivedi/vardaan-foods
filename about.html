Our Story

Rooted in tradition, crafted for your well-being.

The Vardaan Legacy

At Vardaan Foods, we believe that true health begins in the kitchen. For generations, traditional Indian cooking relied on pure, unrefined oils extracted using the "Lakdi Ghani" (wooden pestle) method. Unfortunately, the rise of factory-refined oils has stripped away these natural nutrients, replacing them with harmful chemicals and excessive heat. We set out to change that.

Uncompromising Purity

Our mission is simple: to bring you 100% natural, chemical-free cold-pressed oils. We source the finest, high-density seeds directly from trusted farmers across Rajasthan, UP, and Gujarat. Using authentic wooden Kolhus, we slowly crush the seeds at temperatures below 35°C. This ensures that every drop retains its natural antioxidants, authentic aroma, and vital omega fatty acids.

Why Wood-Pressed?

Modern metal expellers generate intense friction, heating the oil up to 70°C and destroying sensitive nutrients. Our traditional wooden mortar absorbs the friction heat, ensuring a true "cold" press. Furthermore, we never use chemical solvents like Hexane, nor do we bleach or artificially deodorize our oils. What you get is oil in its purest, most potent form—just as nature intended.

Our Commitment

Every bottle of Vardaan Foods oil is a promise of health, transparency, and authenticity. We rigorously lab-test our batches for purity, holding certifications from FSSAI and NABL. We stand by our products because we feed them to our own families. Welcome to the Vardaan family.
