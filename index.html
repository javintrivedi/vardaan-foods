Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Wooden Kolhu and Normal Cold Pressed Oil? +

Normal cold-pressed machines often use metal screws which generate friction heat up to 60°C-70°C. Vardaan uses wooden pestle & mortar (Lakdi Ghani), which absorbs friction heat and keeps oil temperature under 35°C, preserving 100% of sensitive antioxidants.

Why does cold-pressed mustard oil smell so strong and pungent? +

The sharp aroma and pungency come from naturally occurring Allyl Isothiocyanate, a potent antioxidant known for antibacterial and heart-protecting properties. Refined oils strip this out with chemical deodorizers.

Is A2 Gir Cow Ghee made via Bilona process? +

Yes! Our A2 Ghee is prepared using the traditional 2-way wooden churn (Bilona) from pure A2 curd, not raw cream. This yields rich golden granules and high medicinal value according to Ayurveda.
