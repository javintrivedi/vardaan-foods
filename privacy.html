Privacy Policy

How we protect your data and privacy.

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order on Vardaan Foods, we collect personal information necessary to fulfill your order and improve your experience. This includes your name, billing and shipping address, email address, and phone number. We may also collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and interaction with our website through cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data primarily to process transactions, deliver your orders securely, and provide customer support. Additionally, we may use your information to send order updates, promotional offers, and newsletters, provided you have opted in. Your browsing data helps us optimize our website performance and user interface.

3. Data Protection and Security

Vardaan Foods is committed to ensuring that your information is secure. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols and secure servers to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or data leaks. We do not store your credit card information; all payments are processed securely by our trusted third-party payment gateways.

4. Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and will never sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share necessary data with trusted service providers (e.g., shipping carriers, payment processors) explicitly for fulfilling your orders. These partners are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for the deletion of your data. If you have any concerns regarding your privacy or wish to exercise these rights, please contact our support team.
